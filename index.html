About me

Strategy Meets Technical Depth

I am a technology consultant and technical software team leader with a career spanning academia, public sector, and private industry. With a PhD in Software from the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya and over 25 years navigating complex environments, I bring a rare combination of deep technical knowledge and organisational leadership.

My work ranges from directing government-scale technology programmes — including national cybersecurity infrastructure and R&D policy — to leading agile product teams at early-stage startups. I specialise in structuring development processes, fostering team cohesion, and connecting technical vision with real-world delivery.

I believe good software is built by well-led teams with clear processes. My goal is always to leave teams more capable than I found them.
